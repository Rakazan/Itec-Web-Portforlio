🏎️SWS Leagues🏎️

🌎Sodi World Series (SWS) is a world-class series featuring more than 110,000 drivers from 70 countries.🌎

🏆Collect points to compete in the SWS World Grand Final.🏆

🌎Check your stats on the Sodi World Series website and compare yourself to drivers from all over the world!🌎

The SWS Junior Sprint league is offered to drivers from 10 to 14 years old (52" minimum).

The SWS Senior Sprint League is offered to intermediate riders 15 years of age and older.

The SWS Rotax League is offered to advanced riders 16 years of age and older.

The Endurance Cup league represents an opportunity for drivers aged 15 and over to take part in a completely different race format, in teams of 2 to 4 drivers.
